# Restoration direction

The redesign must preserve the original rus-drama.ru visual identity: the original arch-and-bell masthead, the exact theatre name «Московский Театр Русской Драмы под руководством Михаила Щепенко», the original festival identity, the vertical/sectional hierarchy of the legacy site, and the original content structure. Improvements are limited to typography, alignment, spacing, responsive/mobile layout, accessibility, and technical quality. Do not replace the heritage masthead with unrelated stage imagery or invented logos.
